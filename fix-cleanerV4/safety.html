USE WITH CARE

Safety first


Please read the product label and follow the recommended directions before using any Fix Cleaner product.

IMPORTANT

Cleaning products should always be handled responsibly. Use Fix Cleaner products only for their intended purpose and according to the instructions provided on the product packaging.

01. Read before use

Read the product label carefully before using the product. Follow the recommended usage instructions and precautions.

02. Keep away from children

Keep cleaning products out of the reach of children and pets. Do not allow children to handle or use cleaning products.

03. Avoid contact with eyes

Avoid direct contact with eyes and prolonged contact with skin. Wash thoroughly with water if accidental contact occurs.

04. Do not ingest

Cleaning products are not intended for consumption. Do not swallow or intentionally inhale the product.

05. Do not mix products

Do not mix Fix Cleaner with other cleaning products unless the product label specifically says it is safe to do so.

06. Store properly

Keep the container properly closed and store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and unsuitable heat sources.

BEFORE CLEANING

Small precautions can make everyday cleaning safer and easier.

01. Check the surface

When using a cleaning product on a new or delicate surface, test it on a small, inconspicuous area first.

02. Follow the label

Use the recommended amount and method specified on the product packaging.

03. Keep the area ventilated

Maintain suitable ventilation while cleaning, especially when working in enclosed spaces.
