Before you clean

Always read the product label first. Do not mix cleaning products unless the label specifically says it is safe to do so.

Good cleaning habits

  • Keep cleaning products away from children and pets.
  • Use only on surfaces for which the product is intended.
  • Follow the label for dilution, contact time and rinsing instructions.
  • For a new or delicate surface, test a small inconspicuous area first.
  • Wash your hands after cleaning and keep the container closed after use.

The product label is the final source for product-specific usage, dilution and safety directions.
